# Data licence and source

The datasets in this repository (`data/`, `raw/`, `files/`) are **not** an
original work of this repository's author. They are a reformatted copy of open
government data published by the **Swiss Federal Audit Office**
(Eidgenössische Finanzkontrolle, EFK).

## Source

- **Portal (where this data is actually retrieved from):**
  <https://politikfinanzierung.efk.admin.ch>
- **Official open-data catalogue record:** *Politikfinanzierung* on opendata.swiss —
  <https://opendata.swiss/en/dataset/politikfinanzierung>
- **Publisher / data owner:** Eidgenössische Finanzkontrolle (EFK)
- **First published:** 1 September 2023

> **Note on opendata.swiss.** The opendata.swiss entry is a *catalogue record*
> only: its single resource ("Export of the Dataset", XLS) links straight back to
> the EFK portal's exports page (`.../app/de/exports/`). opendata.swiss does not
> host a consolidated downloadable file, and it references only the bulk *exports*
> section. This repository therefore retrieves the data **directly from the EFK
> portal's API** and additionally consolidates themes that are not offered as any
> ready-made dataset anywhere (individual forms, allowances, actors, candidates,
> relationship tables, all three languages, and the full form-file archive).

## Terms of use

On opendata.swiss this dataset is classified as **"Open use"**, the most
permissive of the four Swiss OGD terms. Quoted verbatim from
<https://opendata.swiss/en/terms-of-use>:

> **Open use.**
> You may use this dataset for non-commercial purposes.
> You may use this dataset for commercial purposes.
> You are recommended to provide the source (author, title and link to the
> dataset).

In other words, redistribution — including publicly and for commercial
purposes — is expressly permitted, and providing the source is recommended but
not mandatory. This repository provides the attribution below as good practice.

## Legal basis of the underlying data

The data is collected and published by the EFK under the Swiss transparency
rules for political financing (the transparency provisions of the Federal Act
on Political Rights and its ordinance). Publication of the disclosed figures —
including the names of contributors and candidates — is a statutory
transparency measure carried out by the federal authority.

See also the EFK legal framework:
<https://www.efk.admin.ch/en/legal-framework/>.
