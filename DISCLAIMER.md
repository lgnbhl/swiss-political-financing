## No warranty; authoritative source

The data here is provided **"as is"**, without any warranty as to accuracy,
completeness, or timeliness. It is produced by an automated script and may
contain transformation errors or lag behind the source. For any authoritative
or legal purpose, always rely on the official portal:
<https://politikfinanzierung.efk.admin.ch>.

## Personal data

The dataset contains **personal data** — in particular the names of donors and
of election candidates — that was lawfully published by the EFK as a statutory
political-transparency measure. This repository redistributes that data **for
the same transparency purpose**: to make the officially published information
easier to analyse for research, journalism and public-interest scrutiny.

If you reuse this data, you remain responsible for complying with applicable
data-protection law (in Switzerland the revised **Federal Act on Data
Protection (FADP/revDSG)**; the **GDPR** where it applies to you). In
particular:

- Use the data consistently with its **original transparency purpose**.
- Do **not** use it to profile, target, contact, or harass individuals, or for
  advertising or other unrelated purposes.
- Be aware the EFK may remove or stop publishing individual records after the
  retention period set by law; this mirror is a snapshot and may retain data
  after it disappears at the source.

## Reporting a concern / takedown

If you are an affected person or a rights holder and believe something should be
corrected or removed, please open an issue in this repository. Legitimate
requests — for example records the EFK has taken down at source — will be
handled in good faith and promptly.

One limit is worth stating plainly rather than discovering later. The datasets
under `data/` are rebuilt and committed weekly, so this repository's Git history
contains earlier snapshots. Removing a record takes it out of the current
dataset and out of the published site, which is what determines what anyone
reading or downloading the data actually gets — but the earlier commits still
exist, and purging those requires rewriting the repository's history. Where a
request genuinely needs that, say so and it will be considered.
